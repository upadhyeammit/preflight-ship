## Data Ownership

- What is the source of truth?
- Can multiple services modify this data?
- Who resolves conflicts?
- Is ownership documented?

## Growth

- What happens at 10x scale?
- What happens at 100M rows?
- What happens after 3 years?

## Migrations

- Can migration occur without downtime?
- Can migration be rolled back?
- Can old and new schemas coexist?

## Consistency

- Is eventual consistency acceptable?
- Is strong consistency required?
- What happens during partial failures?
