## Purpose

Review completed designs before implementation.

Validate that discovery output and proposed design are ready for delivery planning.

---

## When to Use

After Engineering Discovery and before Delivery Planning.

Workflow:

Discovery → Architecture Review → Delivery Planning

---

## Phase 1 - Design Validation

Review the proposed design against discovery findings.

Determine:

- Is the design understandable?
- Is the design maintainable?
- Is the design scalable?
- Is the design secure?
- Is the design observable?
- Are service boundaries appropriate?
- Are integration patterns appropriate?

---

## Phase 2 - Checklist Review

Load relevant checklists from `Checklists/` based on the design scope.

Apply only relevant checklists. Explain why each is selected.

Available checklists:

- microservice.md
- rest-api.md
- kafka.md
- database.md
- query-performance.md
- security.md
- performance.md
- scalability.md
- observability.md
- maintainability.md
- dry-design.md

---

## Phase 3 - Tradeoff Analysis

For each significant design decision:

- Available options
- Tradeoffs
- Recommendation

Capture unresolved concerns and open questions.

---

## Phase 4 - Readiness Assessment

Determine whether the design is ready for decision locking and delivery planning.

---
